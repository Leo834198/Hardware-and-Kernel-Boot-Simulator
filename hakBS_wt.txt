Hardware and Kernel Boot Simulator

hakBS is a series of .exe releases (alongside .bat files) that simulate kernel startup, hardware operations, the Power-On Self-Test (POST), and similar processes.


In case you didn't know, you can see how the bootloader loads the kernel, the Power-On Self-Test, hardware operations, and much more.
